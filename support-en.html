Gridolu – Help & Support

Questions, feedback or found a bug? Write to us: support@gridolu.com

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Every day everyone plays with the same pieces and rune offers. You have one attempt. If you quit or close the app, your last score counts. At midnight (your local time) there is a new challenge.

What are runes?

When you reach a score goal, you pick one of three runes. Each rune changes the rules until the end of the round – for example more points, longer combos or rotating pieces.

I bought Gridolu Plus, but it is not active.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases (or in the Plus screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the app under Settings → Privacy settings and in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, locally on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted as well.

Can I play with VoiceOver or with one hand?

Yes. Gridolu is fully playable with VoiceOver. In Settings → Accessibility you can also turn on "Tap instead of drag", color marks and the one-handed mode.
