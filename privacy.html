2. In short

Gridolu does not need an account and does not run its own servers. Almost everything stays on your device. Only the online features Live Challenge, Boss of the Week and the festival World Goal share a player name (or only a random ID) and scores with other players via Apple iCloud (section 8). You can delete these entries yourself in the app at any time. In addition, data leaves your device through Apple's App Store and Game Center and – if you agree, or as far as legally permitted – through the advertising network Google AdMob.

3. Data stored only on your device

Scores and records, saved games, Daily Challenge results and your streak, daily missions, stars, the progress of Grido's island, world map progress, friendships with the animals, the daily gift, festival rewards, settings (sound, haptics, block design, tutorial status) and the status of "Gridolu Plus". This data is not transferred to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

Photo Puzzle: If you choose your own photo, the app uses the iOS photo picker. Gridolu only receives the one picture you select – no access to your library. The picture is stored only on your device and is not transferred to us or to any server.

Sending a photo riddle: If you send a photo as a riddle to friends, the app creates a small file ("….gridolu") with the downsized photo, your name and your message. You send this file yourself via the iOS share sheet (e.g. Messages or AirDrop) – it only goes to the person you choose. We receive nothing.

Widget and Live Activity: The widget (Daily Challenge) and the Live Activity (Boss of the Week) show data from the app on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. For this, the data is only passed on within your device.

4. App Store and in-app purchase (Apple)

Download, updates and the purchase of "Gridolu Plus" are handled entirely by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). We do not receive any payment or contact details, only the information whether a purchase exists. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Gridolu submits your best scores to leaderboards and unlocked achievements to Game Center. Your Game Center name and scores may be visible to other players – you control this in the iOS Settings under Game Center. The provider is Apple; Apple's privacy policy applies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The game works normally without Game Center.

6. Advertising with Google AdMob

Gridolu is free and financed by ads. For this we use Google AdMob by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). When loading and showing ads, Google may process in particular: IP address (to determine the approximate location), device and advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your permission), information about ad impressions and interactions, technical device and performance data, and crash reports.

Consent: After the tutorial we ask for your consent to personalized ads and to accessing information on your device using Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). In addition, iOS asks via "App Tracking Transparency" whether the advertising identifier may be used. Without your consent, at most non-personalized or limited ads are delivered, as far as legally permitted. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings and in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Transfers to third countries: Google may also transfer data to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses are also used.

Rewarded videos ("Second chance (Video)", "Reroll (Video)") are voluntary; the same information applies. With "Gridolu Plus" no more interstitial ads are shown.

More information: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

7. Sharing

When you share something – e.g. your challenge result, your Jelly Pass (image with player name, record, stars and animal friends), a finished photo puzzle or a challenge link – the iOS share sheet opens. The content only goes to the app or person you choose. We receive nothing.

8. Online features via Apple iCloud (Live Challenge, Boss of the Week, World Goal)

For these features the app stores entries in the public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). You need to be signed in to iCloud on your device; the app does not get access to your Apple ID, email address or any other iCloud data. Only a random ID (not your Apple ID) is stored, together with the player name you chose where stated below.

  • Live Challenge: player name, random ID, score, number of moves, score history and cheer emojis you sent. Everyone who has the link to this challenge can see this. The app automatically deletes your Live entries 30 days after the challenge.
  • Boss of the Week: player name, random ID and your damage in this week. All players can see this in the weekly hero list. The app automatically deletes entries of past weeks the next time it is opened.
  • Festival World Goal: during a festival (e.g. Advent or Cherry Blossom) all players collect together. Only a random ID and the number of lines you cleared during this festival are stored – no player name. Others only see the total and the number of players. The app automatically deletes entries of past festivals.

Protection against inappropriate names: Offensive player names are filtered automatically. Using "…" next to a name you can hide a player on your device or report them to us by email. We review reported entries and delete them in case of violations.

Delete it yourself: In the app under Settings → Your data → Delete my online data you remove all your entries immediately; your device then gets a new random ID. Alternatively, we delete them on request by email.

The provider is Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you chose). Without these features no data is stored in iCloud.

9. Children

Gridolu is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond what is described above. Please do not use your full real name as player name.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and to withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Apart from the iCloud entries (section 8) we do not store any data about you ourselves. For advertising data please contact Google directly, for Game Center and purchases Apple; we are happy to help with questions and deletion requests by email to support@gridolu.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de).

11. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
